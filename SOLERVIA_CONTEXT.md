# Solervia Context

Solervia is the company that owns PedidoFlow and other independent SaaS
products. PedidoFlow has its own repository, architecture, database,
deployment, development tasks, and customer-data boundary.

## Portfolio Principles

- Products do not share customer databases by default.
- Prefer free tiers and infrastructure that can scale to zero for dormant
  products.
- Avoid duplicate paid providers and unnecessary vendor fragmentation.
- Do not weaken client isolation or security to consolidate cost.
- Escalate a decision when it creates a shared company standard, a recurring
  portfolio cost, or infrastructure another Solervia product would depend on.
- Extract shared libraries only after real reuse appears in multiple products.

The complete company context preserved in `PEDIDOFLOW_MASTER.md` is the current
authority until a separate Solervia HQ registry is connected.
